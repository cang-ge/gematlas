# Optical Phenomena

Beyond base color, gems exhibit distinct optical phenomena through internal structure. The seven below are the most representative and observable during identification:

## Start with Observation

| Phenomenon | Suggested observation | Structural clue to look for |
|---|---|---|
| Asterism (Star Effect) | Use a point light and slowly rotate the cabochon | Oriented needles or tubes |
| Chatoyancy (Cat's-Eye Effect) | Move the light or cabochon and track the band | Parallel fibers, tubes, or channels |
| Color Change | Compare daylight with a warm incandescent source | Source spectrum and selective absorption |
| Play of Color | Tilt slowly and watch flashes move across the surface | Ordered silica-sphere domains |
| Adularescence (Moonstone Sheen) | Rotate the cabochon and watch the floating sheen | Alternating feldspar lamellae |
| Labradorescence (Spectrolite) | Change the angle and observe metallic flashes | Compositional feldspar lamellae |
| Aventurescence (Sunstone Glitter) | Tilt the gem so platelets reflect in sequence | Oriented metallic platelets |

## Seven Major Phenomena

| Phenomenon | One-line Summary |
|---|---|
| [Asterism (Star Effect)](asterism) | Parallel needle inclusions reflect light into star rays |
| [Chatoyancy (Cat's-Eye Effect)](chatoyancy) | Parallel fibers or tubes form a single bright band |
| [Color Change](color-change) | Gem shows distinctly different colors under different light sources |
| [Play of Color](play-of-color) | Angle-dependent spectral flashes shift across the surface |
| [Adularescence (Moonstone Sheen)](adularescence) | Layered internal structure produces soft, floating blue sheen |
| [Labradorescence (Spectrolite)](labradorescence) | Layered internal structure produces vivid metallic iridescence |
| [Aventurescence (Sunstone Glitter)](aventurescence) | Metallic platelets cause sparkly reflections inside the gem |

An observed phenomenon describes an optical appearance, not a final identification by itself. Lighting, cut, viewing angle, and inclusions all affect the result.
