# Play of Color

## Overview

Play of color is opal's signature visual phenomenon. When white light enters ordered domains formed by
silica spheres, different wavelengths diffract and interfere; changing the viewing angle shifts the
spectral flashes across the surface. Strength, color range, and distribution depend on structural order,
body tone, cut, and lighting.


## Mechanism

Diffraction and interference within ordered silica-sphere domains create angle-dependent spectral flashes.
As the viewing direction changes, different wavelengths are reinforced or suppressed, so the visible color
pattern appears to move. “Play of color” describes the appearance, not a base body color or a single-source sparkle.


## Example Gemstones

| Gem | Microstructure | Color Expression |
|---|---|---|
Precious Opal / 贵欧泊 | ordered silica-sphere domains | angular spectral flashes
Black Opal / 黑欧泊 | dark body tone + silica-sphere domains | red, green, blue
White Opal / 白欧泊 | silica-sphere domains | green, blue, orange
