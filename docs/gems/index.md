# The Stone Index

> 60 species · 6 editorial groups · independent stone records

GemAtlas is built around the stones themselves. Browse all 60 species by name, editorial group, mineral family, crystal system, or hardness, then open an individual record for its physical identity, optical behaviour, treatments, origins, history, and references. Each record also links back to the classification coordinates that explain it.
