# By Maison

## Overview

Seven legendary maisons — Cartier, Van Cleef & Arpels, Boucheron,
Tiffany, Harry Winston, Graff, Chaumet — each defined an era with
signature craftsmanship and aesthetics.


## Core Principles

- Cartier (1847): Art Deco carved-gem suites, panther motif, Love bracelet.
- Van Cleef & Arpels (1906): Mystery Set invisible setting, Alhambra.
- Boucheron (1858): Question Mark necklace, Plume de Paon — nature pioneer.
- Tiffany & Co. (1837): invented the six-prong ring, Blue Book high jewelry.
- Harry Winston (1932): Cluster setting, the "King of Diamonds".
- Graff (1960): record fancy-colour diamond cutting (Venus, Lesedi La Rona).
- Chaumet (1780): Napoleonic court jewelry, Joséphine tiara, Liens.
