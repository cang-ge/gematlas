# Object Records & Image Evidence

## Overview

One image does not always equal one independent work: it may be a display photograph,
historical illustration, another state of the same stone, or a related view created for
studying craft. This page gives a basic method for reading gallery records.


## Core Principles

- Separate the object source from the image source: one answers “what is this?” and the other records who made the image and how it may be reused.
- A year may describe the object, design, or a recutting event; it is not automatically the photograph date.
- Cards marked “Archive relation” may share an object or image. Read them as linked records, not duplicate independent works.
- Weight, history, and institutional custody are archive facts; they do not replace a modern identification, grading report, or valuation.
