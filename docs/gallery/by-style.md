# By Style Era

## Overview

From 19th-century romanticism to contemporary high jewelry — Art
Nouveau's organic curves, Art Deco's geometry, Modernist restraint —
each style mirrors its era.


## Core Principles

- Romanticism (Pre-1900): floral carving, cameos, pearls — Victorian & Edwardian.
- Art Nouveau (1890–1910): whiplash curves, dragonfly tiaras, wheat motifs.
- Art Deco (1920–1935): geometry, black-onyx/diamond contrast, carved gems.
- Mid-Century (1950–1980): Cubism, exotic themes (Indian/Chinese motifs).
- Contemporary (2000+): innovative settings, organic forms, colour high jewelry.
