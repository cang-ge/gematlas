# Gallery

The gallery views jewelry works and legendary-stone archives through four lenses — the great
maisons, the style eras that shaped design, object records and image evidence, and the legendary
stones whose fame outlived their owners. Together with the GalleryGrid's 21 maison records and
five legendary stones, this forms a learning path from workshop to object to historical image.


## Gallery Topics

| Topic | Summary |
|---|---|
| [By Maison](by-house) | Seven legendary maisons — Cartier, Van Cleef & Arpels, Boucheron, Tiffany, Harry Winston, Graff, Chaumet — each defined an era with signature craftsmanship and aesthetics. |
| [By Style Era](by-style) | From 19th-century romanticism to contemporary high jewelry — Art Nouveau's organic curves, Art Deco's geometry, Modernist restraint — each style mirrors its era. |
| [Object Records & Image Evidence](archive-reading) | One image does not always equal one independent work: it may be a display photograph, historical illustration, another state of the same stone, or a related view created for studying craft. This page gives a basic method for reading gallery records. |
| [Legendary Stones](legendary-stones) | A few stones outlive their owners through size, colour, or history — the Hope blue diamond, Koh-i-Noor, the Cullinans, the Regent, the Star of India. Their stories are as precious as the gems themselves. |
