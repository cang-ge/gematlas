# Synthetics & Imitations

## Overview

Synthetics (same chemistry — flux ruby, hydrothermal emerald, CVD
diamond) and imitations (different chemistry — glass, cubic zirconia,
doublets) demand different identification routes.


## Core Principles

- Synthetic ruby/sapphire: flux curved striae and bubbles; hydrothermal has platinum flakes.
- CVD/HPHT diamond: chemically identical to natural — needs UV banding + spectroscopy.
- Cubic zirconia: SG 5.65, excessive dispersion, sharp facet edges — clearly not diamond.
- Glass imitations: bubbles, swirls, flow lines; RI usually 1.5–1.7.

## Examples

| Gem | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| diamond | CZ (cubic zirconia) | SG 5.65 vs 3.52 |
