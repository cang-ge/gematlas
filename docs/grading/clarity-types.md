# Clarity Inclusion Types

## Overview

The type, position, quantity, and visibility of inclusions and blemishes
contribute to clarity descriptions. The same feature can matter differently
across species and must be read with position, durability, and conditions.

## Core Principles

- Feather: fracture; durability impact depends on position, surface reach, direction, size, and species.
- Cloud: hazy cluster; degrades transparency if dense.
- Crystal / mineral: trapped inclusions (rutile, zircon, calcite).
- Needle: rutile silk; gives sapphire its velvety look.
- Blemish: surface chip / scratch / pit.
- Location changes visibility, clarity interpretation, and durability risk; no single multiplier applies.

## Examples

| Gem | Grade | Note |
|---|---|---|
| emerald | jardin (3-phase) | Species-specific inclusion description; read with transparency and durability |
| ruby | silk (rutile needles) | May relate to asterism; read with orientation, density, and cut |
