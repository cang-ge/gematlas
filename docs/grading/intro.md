# Grading

Grading organizes a stone's weight, appearance, and report characteristics
into comparable descriptions. Identification and disclosure support claims
about identity, treatment, and authenticity.
This module covers the GIA 4C diamond framework (Carat / Color / Clarity /
Cut), the hue-tone-saturation model for coloured gems, and the boundaries
between clarity features, treatment disclosure, and origin opinions.


<aside class="gem-identification-boundary gem-module-boundary" aria-labelledby="grading-boundary-en">

  <h2 id="grading-boundary-en">Reading Boundary</h2>
  <p>This module teaches grading descriptions, report terms, and evidence boundaries; it is not an online grading, valuation, or specimen-identification tool.
Grading alone cannot establish natural/synthetic status, treatment, geographic origin, or market price. Formal reports and disclosures depend on the applicable laboratory, rules, and jurisdiction.
</p>

## Grading Topics

| Topic | Summary |
|---|---|
| [GIA 4Cs (Diamond)](diamond-4cs) | A diamond-grading framework established by GIA in 1953 and widely adopted. Four independent measurements—carat, color, clarity, cut—jointly determine a polished diamond's market value. |
| [Coloured-Stone Grading](colored-stones) | Coloured gems lack a single 4C scheme. Instead, colour is graded on three axes—hue (position on the colour wheel), tone (lightness), and saturation (intensity). Many laboratories use these descriptive dimensions, but report terms and thresholds can differ. |
| [Clarity Inclusion Types](clarity-types) | The type, position, quantity, and visibility of inclusions and blemishes contribute to clarity descriptions. The same feature can matter differently across species and must be read with position, durability, and conditions. |
| [Origin & Treatment Disclosure](origin-disclosure) | Treatment status and origin opinions belong to disclosure and laboratory-report contexts. Requirements depend on jurisdiction, rules, reports, and transaction context; they are not one grading scale. |
