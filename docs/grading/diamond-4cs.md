# GIA 4Cs (Diamond)

## Overview

A diamond-grading framework established by GIA in 1953 and widely adopted.
Four independent measurements—carat, color, clarity, cut—jointly
determine a polished diamond's market value.

## Core Principles

- Carat (ct): 1 ct = 0.2 g; 1 point = 0.01 ct. Weight is a measurement, not a universal price or rarity formula.
- Color: D–Z scale, only the yellow/brown tint. D = colorless; Z = light.
- Clarity: FL–I3 has 11 grades under 10×; inclusions and blemishes determine the grade.
- Cut: Excellent → Poor. Proportions, symmetry, polish, brightness, fire, and scintillation must be read together.
