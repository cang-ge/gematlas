# GIA 4Cs (Diamond)

## Overview

The world standard for diamond grading, established by GIA in 1953.
Four independent measurements—carat, color, clarity, cut—jointly
determine a polished diamond's market value.


## Core Principles

- Carat (ct): 1 ct = 0.2 g; 1 point = 0.01 ct. Rarity rises exponentially.
- Color: D–Z scale, only the yellow/brown tint. D = colorless; Z = light.
- Clarity: FL–I3 scale under 10×. Inclusions and blemishes determine grade.
- Cut: Excellent → Poor. Proportions, symmetry, polish drive brilliance.
