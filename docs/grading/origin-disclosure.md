# Origin & Treatment Disclosure

## Overview

Treatment status and origin opinions belong to disclosure and laboratory-report
contexts. Requirements depend on jurisdiction, rules, reports, and transaction
context; they are not one grading scale.


<aside class="gem-identification-boundary" aria-labelledby="identification-boundary-origin-disclosure-en">
  <p class="gem-identification-boundary__eyebrow">GRADING BOUNDARY</p>

  <p>This page separates treatment disclosure from origin opinions; it does not provide legal advice, price forecasts, or a final disclosure conclusion for a specimen. Any “must disclose” statement must be tied to a jurisdiction, rule, report, and transaction context; high-value transactions should use qualified laboratory and professional advice.
</p>
</aside>

## Core Principles

- “No Indications of Treatment” means no treatment indications were observed; it is not absolute proof of never-treated, and price is not determined by this alone.
- Routine treatment (e.g. heat on sapphire / ruby): disclosure depends on the applicable rules, report, and transaction context.
- Enhancement (oil/resin in emerald, dye, fracture filling): explain stability, care, report wording, and disclosure scope together.
- Origin opinion: based on inclusion suites and trace chemistry, may be inconclusive, and is not a grade.

## Examples

| Gem | Grade | Note |
|---|---|---|
| ruby | heated (H) | Treatment-disclosure example; does not set a price cap alone |
| emerald | minor oil (Cedarwood) | Read with stability, care, report wording, and applicable rules |
| sapphire | unheated, Kashmir | No treatment indication and origin opinion are separate dimensions |
