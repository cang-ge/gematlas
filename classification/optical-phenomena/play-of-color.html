Play of Color ​

Overview ​

Play of color is opal's signature visual phenomenon. When white light enters ordered domains formed by silica spheres, different wavelengths diffract and interfere; changing the viewing angle shifts the spectral flashes across the surface. Strength, color range, and distribution depend on structural order, body tone, cut, and lighting.

Mechanism ​

Diffraction and interference within ordered silica-sphere domains create angle-dependent spectral flashes. As the viewing direction changes, different wavelengths are reinforced or suppressed, so the visible color pattern appears to move. “Play of color” describes the appearance, not a base body color or a single-source sparkle.
