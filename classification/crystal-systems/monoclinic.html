Monoclinic ​

Overview ​

The monoclinic system has three unequal axes with two meeting at right angles and the third (b-axis) inclined. Crystals are biaxial. The dominant cleavage is parallel to the (010) pinacoid. Jade (nephrite) and most feldspars fall here.
