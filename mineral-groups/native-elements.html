Native Elements ​

Overview ​

Native-element minerals are crystals of a single element. In gemology, the most important native element is diamond (carbon), formed by sp³-hybridized carbon atoms in a tetrahedral covalent network — the hardest known natural material. Other native elements (gold, silver, platinum) are typically used as ornamental or precious-metal work rather than faceted gems.

Formula Class ​

Single-element composition

Crystal Structure ​

Atoms occupy lattice positions in characteristic geometries; metallic native elements (gold, silver) form face-centered cubic structures, while non-metals (diamond) form covalent tetrahedral networks.
